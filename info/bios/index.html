The residents, regulars, travelers, and almost-myths of the bar.

Andrew Woo-jong Lee

Christian, The Bartenderhe/they

Creative

The writing, music, lyric, and direction team behind the world of the show.

Lionel Humphreys

Bookwriterhe/they

Lionel Humphreys improvises with the Stanford Improvisors and made his Stanford playwriting debut on SLOCo's True North: A 48 Hour Musical. He was a writer and director for Gunn Theatre's PIAD in '24 and '25. Outside of Stanford, he has starred in GIFT Theatre's Bye Bye Birdie and multiple Gunn Theatre productions including The Play that Goes Wrong and The Cottage.

Samuel Minev-Benzecry

Co-writerhe/him

Samuel Minev-Benzecry is a senior studying Earth Systems and Linguistics. Originally from Manaus in Northern Brazil, he was inspired by his regional background and family history to co-write the story of Port-Pourri. Samuel's practice is mostly centered on film photography, and Port-Pourri was his first involvement with theater.

Emily Saletan

Lyricistall pronouns

Emily Saletan is an actor, singer, and composer/lyricist who grew up folk dancing in the Boston area and the Pacific Northwest. Emily is a coterminal master's student in Music, Science, and Technology, having studied Symbolic Systems (Neurosciences) in undergrad with minors in Music and Dance. Infinite thanks to her mom, Jill, for loving her so evidently, and to her dad, Tony, for his compassionate legacy of music education. www.emilysaletan.com
